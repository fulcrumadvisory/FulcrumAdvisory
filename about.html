Background

Practitioner First. Consultant Second.

David Taylor spent over 16 years as VP of Information Systems and CISO inside a FOCI-mitigated defense contractor — not advising from the outside, but operating from within a live, DCSA-scrutinized compliance environment with real consequences for getting it wrong.

That means every recommendation he makes comes from direct experience: running DCSA engagements, executing SSA and mitigation agreements, building CMMC-scoped system security plans, and deploying AI tooling inside GCC High environments where the compliance boundary is real and the cost of a misstep is a contract, a clearance, or both.

Fulcrum Advisory was built on the conviction that the defense industrial base deserves advisors who have lived the frameworks they sell — not just studied them. That difference shows up immediately in the quality and specificity of the guidance.
