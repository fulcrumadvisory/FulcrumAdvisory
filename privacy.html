Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2025

Fulcrum Advisory ("we," "us," or "our") operates this website at fulcrumadvisory.us. This privacy policy describes how we handle information submitted through this website.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily submit through our contact form, including your name, email address, organization, and the content of your message. We do not use tracking cookies, behavioral analytics, or advertising networks. If we add analytics in the future (such as Google Analytics), this policy will be updated before any tracking is enabled.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your contact details and message content are not used for any purpose other than responding to you directly.

Data Storage

Contact form submissions are processed and stored by our form handling provider. We retain inquiry information only as long as reasonably necessary to follow up on active inquiries. We do not maintain a marketing database or CRM populated from website inquiries without your explicit consent.

Third-Party Services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google Fonts requests are served from Google's CDN and are subject to Google's privacy policy. We use Google Fonts solely for typography rendering and do not share any personal information with Google through this integration.

If you schedule a call through Calendly (when the Calendly integration is active), your scheduling information is subject to Calendly's privacy policy.

Security

This website is served over HTTPS. We implement reasonable technical measures to protect information in transit. However, no transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information you submit.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have submitted to us. To make such a request, contact us at info@fulcrumadvisory.us. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's Privacy

This website is directed to professional audiences in the defense contracting sector. We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under 18 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as our practices change. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of this website after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
