Executive Summary

Fulcrum Advisory Technologies, LLC is a defense-sector advisory and software company founded by David Taylor — a 25-year enterprise IT and cybersecurity veteran with 16+ years operating as VP of Information Systems and CISO inside a live FOCI-mitigated defense contractor. The company operates at the intersection of three converging market forces: the Section 847 DFARS rulemaking expanding FOCI compliance requirements to ~37,740 previously unaffected contractors, the CMMC final rule now in mandatory enforcement, and the explosion of AI deployment in GCC High environments.

The business comprises two interlocking units: a professional advisory practice (FOCI, CMMC, AI/GCC High, vCISO) and FulcrumFOCI — the first and, as of this writing, only purpose-built FOCI compliance management platform on the market. FulcrumFOCI is a licensed SaaS solution built on Microsoft Power Platform, deployed directly into the customer's Azure Government or GCC High tenant. It manages the full FOCI compliance lifecycle — from SF-328 assessment through annual GSC reports, self-inspections, visitor management, changed condition tracking, and DCSA inspection readiness.

The timing is extraordinary: the proposed DFARS rule published May 7, 2026, will convert a niche compliance obligation affecting ~2,000 cleared facilities into a mainstream requirement affecting 41,000 companies covering $200 billion in DoD acquisitions. No incumbent software platform exists built for this use case. Fulcrum is positioned to own the category before it forms.

2.2 Deployment Model & Technical Differentiation

FulcrumFOCI deploys as a managed Power Platform solution into the customer's existing Azure Government or GCC High tenant. This is a critical differentiator: ECP, TCP, and other CUI-adjacent FOCI documents never leave the customer's compliance boundary. There is no shared multi-tenant SaaS infrastructure, no FedRAMP obligation on Fulcrum, and the customer's existing CMMC posture is completely unaffected.

The solution is license-gated via JWT, allowing Fulcrum to manage subscriptions, enforce tier entitlements, and remotely expire access without ever touching the customer's environment. Updates are delivered as versioned managed solution packages — customers run a single PowerShell script to upgrade.

This architecture mirrors how Microsoft's own Power Platform ISV partners deploy (e.g., Brightwork, Inogic, Click Dimensions) and is specifically designed to scale toward AppSource distribution as the partner program matures.

Why This Matters to Buyers
  • Data sovereignty — DCSA-sensitive content stays in the customer's boundary
  • No FedRAMP uplift needed from Fulcrum
  • Leverages Microsoft's existing IL4/IL5 certifications
  • Transparent to CMMC assessors — no new external system to scope
  • Works inside classified air-gap environments with on-premises Power Platform
Technology Stack

Microsoft Power Platform (Dataverse, Power Apps, Power Automate, Power Pages, Power BI, Copilot Studio) · SharePoint Online · Microsoft Entra ID · Azure OpenAI (Tier 3) · JWT license enforcement (RS256)
